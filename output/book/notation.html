Unified Notation and Conventions

This note is the canonical notation registry for the project. Chapters may introduce local symbols, but they must not redefine symbols listed here. When a textbook uses different notation, translate it into these conventions.

Core conventions

  1. Scalars use italic lowercase letters, vectors use bold lowercase letters, and matrices use bold uppercase letters.
  2. Vectors are column vectors unless stated otherwise.
  3. SI units are used in calculations. Angles are expressed in radians.
  4. Coordinate frames are right-handed. A leading left superscript identifies the frame in which coordinates are expressed.
  5. Continuous time uses \(t\); sampled time uses the integer index \(k\).
  6. A wide hat over a twist, as in \(\widehat{\boldsymbol\xi}\), denotes the declared Lie-algebra hat operator and is not an estimate.
  7. Bold symbols and scalar symbols with the same letter are distinct; for example, \(\mathbf R\) is a rotation matrix whereas \(R\) may be a signed turning radius.
  8. A symbol may have a different local meaning only in a clearly separated mathematical domain where its type and meaning are explicitly redeclared; for example, \(m\) may denote a matrix dimension in a generic linear-algebra statement and robot mass in a dynamics model.

Part I: Motion, Mechanics, and Control

Notation introduced in 1  Linear Algebra Foundations

Symbol Meaning, units, and convention
\(a,\alpha\) Scalar quantity.
\(m,n,i,j\) Integer dimensions or indices declared locally.
\(\mathbb R\) Set of real numbers.
\(\mathbb R^n,\mathbb R^{m\times n}\) Real coordinate columns and real matrices with dimensions shown by the superscripts.
\(\mathbb R_{\geq0},\mathbb R_{>0}\) Nonnegative and strictly positive real numbers.
\(\mathbf{v}\in\mathbb{R}^n\) Column vector with \(n\) components.
\(\mathbf{A}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}\) Matrix with \(m\) rows and \(n\) columns.
\(\mathbf{I}_n\) \(n\times n\) identity matrix.
\(\mathbf{0}_n\) Zero vector with \(n\) components.
\((\cdot)^\mathsf{T}\) Transpose.
\((\cdot)^{-1}\) Inverse, when it exists.
\((\cdot)^{-\mathsf T}\) Inverse transpose, \((\mathbf A^{-1})^\mathsf T=(\mathbf A^\mathsf T)^{-1}\) for nonsingular \(\mathbf A\).
\(\lVert\mathbf{v}\rVert_2\) Euclidean norm of \(\mathbf{v}\), with the same units as \(\mathbf v\).
\(\det(\mathbf A)\) Determinant of a square matrix.
\(\operatorname{rank}(\mathbf A)\) Dimension of the column space of \(\mathbf A\).
\(\operatorname{nullity}(\mathbf A)\) Dimension of the null space of \(\mathbf A\).
\(\operatorname{span}(\mathbf v_1,\ldots,\mathbf v_k)\) Set of all linear combinations of the listed vectors.
\(\operatorname{Col}(\mathbf A),\mathcal N(\mathbf A)\) Column space and null space of \(\mathbf A\), which are subspaces of the output and input spaces.
\(\mathbf u\times\mathbf v\) Three-dimensional cross product of vectors expressed in the same right-handed orthonormal frame; its units are the product of the input units.
\(O(n),SO(n)\) Orthogonal group and special orthogonal group of real \(n\times n\) matrices: \(\mathbf Q^\mathsf T\mathbf Q=\mathbf I_n\), and \(SO(n)\) additionally requires \(\det\mathbf Q=1\).

Notation introduced in 2  Geometry and Coordinate Frames

Symbol Meaning, units, and convention
\(\{a\}\) Right-handed coordinate frame named \(a\).
\(O_a\) Origin of frame \(\{a\}\).
\(\mathbf{e}_{x,a},\mathbf{e}_{y,a},\mathbf{e}_{z,a}\) Unit basis vectors of frame \(\{a\}\).
\({}^{a}\mathbf{v}\) Coordinates of geometric vector \(\mathbf{v}\) expressed in frame \(\{a\}\).
\({}^{a}\mathbf{p}_P\) Position of point \(P\) relative to \(O_a\), expressed in frame \(\{a\}\), in \(\mathrm{m}\).
\({}^{a}\mathbf r_{OP}\) Relative-position vector from point \(O\) to point \(P\), expressed in frame \(\{a\}\), in \(\mathrm{m}\).
\(\mathbf{R}_{ab}\) Rotation in \(SO(2)\) or \(SO(3)\) mapping coordinates from frame \(\{b\}\) to frame \(\{a\}\).
\({}^{a}\mathbf{p}_b\) Position of origin \(O_b\) relative to \(O_a\), expressed in frame \(\{a\}\), in \(\mathrm{m}\).
\(\boldsymbol\omega\) Three-dimensional angular-velocity vector in \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).

Notation introduced in 3  Differential-Drive Kinematics and Numerical Integration: Model and Derivation

Symbol Meaning, units, and convention
\(t\) Continuous time in \(\mathrm{s}\).
\(k\in\mathbb N_0\) Sample index, where \(\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\).
\(t_k\) Time at sample \(k\), in \(\mathrm{s}\).
\(\Delta t=t_{k+1}-t_k\) Sampling or numerical-integration interval in \(\mathrm{s}\).
\(\dot{\mathbf{x}},\ddot{\mathbf{x}}\) First and second derivatives with respect to time, with units of the differentiated quantity per \(\mathrm{s}\) and per \(\mathrm{s}^2\).
\(\{w\},\{b\}\) World and robot body frames.
\(O_w,O_b\) Origins of the world and body frames.
\(\mathbf{q}=[x\;y\;\theta]^\mathsf{T}\) Planar robot pose coordinates in the world frame, with component units \(\mathrm{m},\mathrm{m},\mathrm{rad}\).
\(\theta\) Counter-clockwise heading of \(\{b\}\) relative to \(\{w\}\), in \(\mathrm{rad}\).
\(\mathbf u=[v\;\omega]^\mathsf T\) Planar body-velocity input with component units \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).
\(v,\omega\) Signed body-forward speed in \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) and counter-clockwise yaw rate in \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).
\(\phi_L,\phi_R\) Left and right wheel angles in \(\mathrm{rad}\).
\(\dot{\phi}_L,\dot{\phi}_R\) Left and right wheel angular speeds in \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).
\(v_L,v_R\) Signed longitudinal ground speeds of the left and right wheels in \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).
\(r,L\) Effective wheel radius and track width between wheel contact lines, in \(\mathrm{m}\).
\(C\) Instantaneous centre of curvature for planar motion.
\(\rho\) Nonnegative radial distance in the declared local geometric context, in \(\mathrm{m}\).
\(R\) Signed turning radius in \(\mathrm{m}\), positive for an ICC on the robot’s left and distinct from rotation matrix \(\mathbf R\).
\(\Delta\ell,\Delta\theta\) Signed arc-length change in \(\mathrm{m}\) and heading change in \(\mathrm{rad}\) over an interval.
\(\mathbf q_k^{\mathrm E}\) Forward Euler approximation of the pose at sample \(k\), with the same component units as \(\mathbf q\).
\(E(\Delta t)\) Declared numerical-integration error magnitude at step size \(\Delta t\).
\(e_p\) One-step position-error magnitude in the declared integration comparison, in \(\mathrm m\).
\(O(h^p)\) Quantity bounded in magnitude by a constant times \(h^p\) as \(h\to0\).
\(\operatorname{sinc}(z)\) Unnormalised sinc function, \(\sin z/z\) for \(z\neq0\) and \(1\) for \(z=0\).

Notation introduced in 6  Planar Rigid-Body Motion: SE(2) and Twists

Symbol Meaning, units, and convention
\({}^{a}\overline{\mathbf p}_P\) Homogeneous point coordinate obtained by appending \(1\) to \({}^{a}\mathbf p_P\); position entries are in \(\mathrm m\) and the final entry is dimensionless.
\(SO(2)\) Special orthogonal group of planar rotation matrices satisfying \(\mathbf R^\mathsf T\mathbf R=\mathbf I_2\) and \(\det\mathbf R=1\).
\(SE(2)\) Special Euclidean group representing planar rotation and translation together.
\(\mathbf{T}_{ab}\) Homogeneous transform in \(SE(2)\) mapping coordinates from frame \(\{b\}\) to frame \(\{a\}\).
\(T_{\mathbf I_2}SO(2)\) Tangent space of \(SO(2)\) at \(\mathbf I_2\): the linear space of rotation-curve derivatives through the identity.
\(T_{\mathbf I_3}SE(2)\) Tangent space of \(SE(2)\) at \(\mathbf I_3\): the linear space of transformation-curve derivatives through the identity.
\(\mathbf S_2\) Planar skew generator \(\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}\).
\(\mathfrak{so}(2)\) Lie algebra and identity tangent space of \(SO(2)\), comprising \(2\times2\) real skew-symmetric matrices.
\(\mathfrak{se}(2)\) Lie algebra and identity tangent space of \(SE(2)\), comprising structured \(3\times3\) infinitesimal planar rigid-motion matrices.
\(\boldsymbol\xi=[\boldsymbol\nu^\mathsf T\;\omega]^\mathsf T\) Planar twist coordinate column with linear part \(\boldsymbol\nu\in\mathbb R^2\) in \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) and angular part \(\omega\in\mathbb R\) in \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).
\(\widehat{\boldsymbol\xi}\) Hat-operator matrix representing \(\boldsymbol\xi\) in \(\mathfrak{se}(2)\); its upper-left block is in \(\mathrm{s^{-1}}\) and its translation column is in \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).
\((\cdot)^\vee\) Vee operator, inverse of the declared hat operator, returning the twist coordinate column.
\(\boldsymbol\xi_b,\boldsymbol\xi_w\) Body and spatial twist coordinates for the same instantaneous motion, using body axes/body origin and world axes/world origin respectively.
\(\boldsymbol\nu_b,\boldsymbol\nu_w\) Linear parts of the body and spatial twists in \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).
\(\operatorname{Ad}_{\mathbf T}\) Adjoint mapping that changes twist coordinates according to rigid transform \(\mathbf T\); its dimensions and mixed units follow the declared twist convention.
\(\ell\) Characteristic length in \(\mathrm m\) used to compare linear and angular twist components.

Notation introduced in 7  Degrees of Freedom and Spatial Motion: SO(3) and SE(3)

Symbol Meaning, units, and convention
\(\mathcal B\) Set of labelled material points belonging to the rigid body.
\(\chi:\mathcal B\to\mathbb R^d\) Placement map assigning a world position in \(\mathrm m\) to every material point in a \(d\)-dimensional model.
\(\mathcal C\) Configuration space: the set of all configurations allowed by the model; it may be curved and need not be a vector space.
\(\chi\in\mathcal C\) One complete physical configuration represented as an admissible placement map.
\(\mathbf q\) Chosen column of generalised coordinates describing a configuration; its component units depend on the system.
\(n_{\mathrm{dof}}\) Number of configuration degrees of freedom.
\(n_v,n_c\) Numbers of scalar variables and independent regular equality constraints in a local count.
\(\mathbf z\in\mathbb R^{n_v}\) Ambient coordinate column used to express equality constraints; its component units depend on the system.
\(\mathbf g:\mathbb R^{n_v}\to\mathbb R^{n_c}\) Equality-constraint mapping, with valid coordinates satisfying \(\mathbf g(\mathbf z)=\mathbf 0_{n_c}\).
\(\mathbf J_{\mathbf g}(\mathbf z)\) \(n_c\times n_v\) constraint Jacobian with entries \(\partial g_i/\partial z_j\); row units depend on the corresponding constraint and coordinate.
\(SO(3)\) Special orthogonal group of spatial rotation matrices satisfying \(\mathbf R^\mathsf T\mathbf R=\mathbf I_3\) and \(\det\mathbf R=1\).
\(\mathbf R_x(\theta),\mathbf R_y(\theta),\mathbf R_z(\theta)\) Active right-hand-rule rotations in \(SO(3)\) about the declared coordinate axes.
\(\mathbf Q_w,\mathbf Q_b\) Finite orientation increments in \(SO(3)\) expressed along world or body axes; premultiply or postmultiply accordingly.
\(\boldsymbol\omega_w,\boldsymbol\omega_b\) Same world-relative angular velocity resolved along world or body axes, in \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).
\(SE(3)\) Special Euclidean group representing spatial rotation and translation together.
\(\mathbf T_{wb}\in SE(3)\) Spatial pose of frame \(\{b\}\) relative to frame \(\{w\}\) and transform from body to world point coordinates; its rotation block is dimensionless and translation block is in \(\mathrm m\).
\([\mathbf v]_\times\) Cross-product matrix satisfying \([\mathbf v]_\times\mathbf y=\mathbf v\times\mathbf y\), with the same units as \(\mathbf v\).
\(\mathfrak{so}(3)\) Lie algebra and identity tangent space of \(SO(3)\), comprising \(3\times3\) real skew-symmetric matrices.
\(\mathbf a_w,\alpha,\boldsymbol\varphi_w=\alpha\mathbf a_w\) Unit rotation axis, signed angle, and rotation-vector coordinates expressed along world axes; the angle and rotation vector are labelled in \(\mathrm{rad}\).
\(\exp(\mathbf A)\) Matrix exponential \(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\mathbf A^j/j!\).
\(\mathsf q=(q_w,\mathbf q_v)\) Quaternion written as an ordered scalar–vector pair.
\(\otimes\) Hamilton product of two quaternions, with order following rotation-composition order.
\(\mathsf q^*=(q_w,-\mathbf q_v)\) Quaternion conjugate; the inverse when \(\mathsf q\) has unit norm.
\(\mathfrak{se}(3)\) Lie algebra and identity tangent space of \(SE(3)\), comprising structured \(4\times4\) infinitesimal spatial rigid-motion matrices.
\(\boldsymbol\xi=[\boldsymbol\nu^\mathsf T\;\boldsymbol\omega^\mathsf T]^\mathsf T\) Spatial twist coordinate column in the project’s linear-first order: three entries in \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) followed by three in \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\).
\(\|\mathbf M\|_{\mathrm F}\) Frobenius norm of a real matrix, with units following its entries.
\(e_{\mathrm{orth}},e_{\det},e_q\) Orthogonality, determinant, and unit-quaternion residuals.
\(\|\boldsymbol\xi\|_\ell\) Application-dependent twist comparison magnitude in \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) after choosing characteristic length \(\ell\).
\(\boldsymbol\Omega_k=[\boldsymbol\omega_{b,k}]_\times\) Body-expressed angular-velocity matrix at sample \(k\), in \(\mathrm{s^{-1}}\).
\(\widetilde{\mathbf R}_{k+1}\) Forward Euler candidate for the next orientation matrix; it is not necessarily in \(SO(3)\).

Notation introduced in 8  Screw Motion, the SE(3) Exponential, and Spatial Pose Integration

Symbol Meaning, units, and convention
\(I,L_i,\mathcal B_i,\bigsqcup_{i\in I}\mathcal B_i\) Finite link-index set, link \(i\), its material-point set, and the disjoint union of all link point sets.
\(c_b:\mathcal B_b\to\mathbb R^3\) Fixed body-coordinate assignment mapping each material point of link \(b\) to its coordinates in frame \(\{b\}\), in \(\mathrm m\).
\(A_{\mathbf T_{wb}}:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3\) Affine point-coordinate map induced by pose matrix \(\mathbf T_{wb}\), with input and output in \(\mathrm m\).
\(\chi_{\mathbf T_{wb}}=A_{\mathbf T_{wb}}\circ c_b\), \(\Phi_b:SE(3)\to\mathcal C_b\) Rigid-link configuration induced by a pose and the one-to-one pose-to-configuration correspondence.
\(\mathcal Q\), \(N\), \(n\), \(j\) Generalised-coordinate domain, number of joints, number of coordinates, and joint index; \(\mathcal Q\) may contain mixed-unit coordinates.
\({}^{a}\mathcal L\), \({}^{a}\mathbf p_\ell\), \({}^{a}\mathbf s\), \(\lambda\) Oriented axis line, a point on it in \(\mathrm m\), its unit positive direction, and signed distance \(\lambda\) in \(\mathrm m\).
\(\theta_j,\dot\theta_j,d_j,\dot d_j\) Revolute angle and rate in \(\mathrm{rad}\) and \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\), and prismatic displacement and rate in \(\mathrm m\) and \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\), for joint \(j\).
\(\mathbf F_e:\mathcal Q\to SE(3)\) Forward-kinematics map returning the pose of selected frame \(\{e\}\) relative to space frame \(\{s\}\); rotation is dimensionless and translation is in \(\mathrm m\).
\(h\) Signed screw pitch in \(\mathrm{m\,rad^{-1}}\): axial travel per positive radian of rotation.
\({}^{a}\mathbf S_R,{}^{a}\mathbf S_H,{}^{a}\mathbf S_P\) Normalised revolute, finite-pitch helical, and prismatic screw-axis coordinate columns expressed in frame \(\{a\}\), using linear-first six-entry order and case-dependent mixed units.
\({}^{a}\mathbf S=[({}^{a}\mathbf s_v)^{\mathsf T}\;({}^{a}\mathbf s_\omega)^{\mathsf T}]^{\mathsf T}\) Normalised screw-axis coordinates with linear and angular coefficient blocks; \(\mathbf s_v\) is in \(\mathrm{m\,rad^{-1}}\) for rotational screws and dimensionless for prismatic screws, while \(\mathbf s_\omega\) is dimensionless.
\(\sigma,\dot\sigma\) Signed motion amount along a normalised screw and its rate: \(\mathrm{rad}\) and \(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\) for rotational screws, or \(\mathrm m\) and \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) for prismatic screws.
\(\widehat{{}^{a}\mathbf S}\) Hat-operator matrix representation of a screw axis in \(\mathfrak{se}(3)\), with mixed units following \({}^{a}\mathbf S\).
\({}^{a}\boldsymbol\eta=[({}^{a}\boldsymbol\rho)^{\mathsf T}\;({}^{a}\boldsymbol\phi)^{\mathsf T}]^{\mathsf T}\) Finite exponential-coordinate column with linear block \(\boldsymbol\rho\) in \(\mathrm m\) and rotation-vector block \(\boldsymbol\phi\) labelled in \(\mathrm{rad}\).
\(\mathbf T_\Delta\) Finite rigid-displacement transformation in \(SE(3)\) produced by a screw exponential or finite twist increment; translation is in \(\mathrm m\).
\(\boldsymbol\Omega=[{}^{a}\mathbf s_\omega]_\times\), \(\mathbf G(\theta)\) Unit-axis cross-product matrix and normalised-screw translation map; \(\mathbf G\) maps \(\mathrm{m\,rad^{-1}}\) to \(\mathrm m\).
\(\operatorname{Log}(\mathbf T)\), \(\log_{\mathrm{sel}}\), \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{sel}}\), \(\log_{\mathrm p}\) Set of all real rigid-motion logarithms, a selected single-valued branch, its domain, and the chapter’s principal branch; logarithms lie in \(\mathfrak{se}(3)\) with finite mixed-unit blocks.
\(\boldsymbol\Phi=[{}^{a}\boldsymbol\phi]_\times\) Cross-product matrix of the finite rotation-vector block, with entries labelled in \(\mathrm{rad}\).
\(A(\theta),B(\theta),C(\theta),D(\theta)\) Continuously extended scalar coefficients used in the rotation, left-Jacobian, and inverse-left-Jacobian formulas.
\(\mathbf J({}^{a}\boldsymbol\phi)\) Dimensionless \(3\times3\) left Jacobian of \(SO(3)\) mapping \(\boldsymbol\rho\) to the finite translation column.
\(c_\theta,{}^{a}\mathbf u,s_\theta,\mathbf Q\) Trace-derived cosine, skew-difference column, sine magnitude, and symmetric axis outer-product matrix used by the principal rotation logarithm.
\(\theta_{\mathrm{series}},\varepsilon_\pi,\varepsilon_{\mathrm{zero}},\varepsilon_{p,0}\) Series-selection, near-\(\pi\), angular-zero, and translation-zero thresholds; the first three are in \(\mathrm{rad}\) and \(\varepsilon_{p,0}\) is in \(\mathrm m\).
\(\|\mathbf v\|_\infty\) Infinity norm: the largest absolute component of a finite column, with the same units as \(\mathbf v\).
\(\widetilde{\mathbf T}\), \(e_{\mathrm{row}}\) Candidate homogeneous transformation and its bottom-row residual before validation; candidate translation is in \(\mathrm m\) and the residual is dimensionless.
\(e_{R,\mathrm{rt}},e_{p,\mathrm{rt}}\) Rotation and translation residuals for an exponential/logarithm round trip, dimensionless and in \(\mathrm m\) respectively.
